      Experience

      Most recent first.

      Samsung Semiconductor India Research

      Bengaluru

      Physical Design Engineer

      May 2025 – Present · Samsung N4 · Project cHBM Nemosyne

      • Own the physical implementation of block MGMTSS (588 memory instances) from floorplanning through CTS, place and route, and full sign-off.
      • Responsible for physical implementation and sign-off of the HBMCSS sub-system.
      • Resolved a critical floorplanning problem in MGMTSS: high memory density caused severe timing pressure, so I devised a placement strategy that closed timing.
      • Fixed congestion in HBMCSS caused by long-hop nets, using targeted floorplan and pin-assignment iterations to remove routing detours and hotspots.
      • Completed physical verification sign-off: DRC, LVS and ERC in Calibre.
      • Completed EMIR sign-off with RedHawk-SC for electromigration and IR-drop compliance.

      Intel Foundry Services

      Bengaluru

      Physical Design Engineer

      Sep 2021 – Apr 2025 · Intel 7, 3 and 18A

      • Owned and delivered more than 8 block-level and top-level designs, including NOC, CMN_tile, MXP, GIC, SWITCH, Channel blocks and HBI (3DIC).
      • Automated placement and routing for about 40K pins, cutting floorplan creation time by 85%.
      • Ran early PDN analysis (IRAP) and TSV planning to find the best block placement.
      • Delivered tape-outs on Intel 7, 3 and 18A with design quality and timing convergence intact.
      • Rewarded for finalising the CTS structure for the Intel3 project after several experiments.
      • Recommended the 210H standard cell library for the GVF IO die, for a 5–7% area reduction on designs under 1.5 GHz with no PPA or DRC impact.
      • Presented the RTL-to-Floorplan (RTL2FP) flow, with insights for hierarchical block and SoC design.
      • Led end-to-end implementation of a high-bandwidth Network-on-Chip (NoC) architecture, and designed and implemented the Hybrid Bond Interface (HBI) for advanced 3DIC chips.

      Microchip Technologies

      Chennai

      Physical Design Engineer

      Dec 2017 – Aug 2021 · TSMC 40nm, UMC 65nm

      Joined as an intern, then trainee, ASIC physical design engineer and engineer.

      • Led power planning and physical verification for a TSMC 40nm automotive Ethernet chip (Carinae). I owned pad-ring placement, PG net derivation, PCF generation and seal-ring integration.
      • Ran static and dynamic power analysis in RedHawk (functional and scan modes, VCD and vectorless). Found and resolved a cross-project electromigration root cause that senior management recognised.
      • Designed and integrated pre-routed analog blocks in Innovus (76 sensitive signals, 7+ analog blocks) on the Wolverhampton MCU32 65nm project, meeting resistance constraints the analog engineers verified.
      • Generated inputs for RedHawk ACE, including memory simulation and IP profiling (Sim2IPROF).
      • Implemented GDS post-processing in Calibre: metal and OD/PO fill, seal ring, Mask-ID and Rev-ID.
      • Contributed to the Trafford2 project (A0 and B0 revisions).
